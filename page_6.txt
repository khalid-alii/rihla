BIT2083 Fundamental of Computational Thinking: Phython 
The main task is to: 
1.​ Identify a real-world sustainability problem. 
2.​ Develop a Python console application that provides a practical solution to the 
identified problem. 
3.​ Explain how the application contributes to the selected SDG(s). 
The application must demonstrate computational thinking concepts and apply Python 
programming techniques learned throughout the course. 
Students may propose their own project idea or select from the examples below. 
SDG 2: Zero Hunger 
●​ Food Donation Management System 
●​ Household Food Waste Tracker 
●​ School Meal Planning Calculator 
●​ Food Inventory Expiry Checker 
●​ Community Food Distribution Planner
