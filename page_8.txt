BIT2083 Fundamental of Computational Thinking: Phython 
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (Minimum) 
The application shall include at least five (5) major functions appropriate to the 
selected problem. Typical functions may include: 
1.​ Display Main Menu 
2.​ Enter or Record Information 
3.​ Perform Calculations or Analysis 
4.​ Generate and Display Results 
5.​ Exit the Application 
Additional functions may be implemented depending on the project requirements.
